Kinetic Innovation

Innovation Idea by Mukul Bhoi

WALKWATT revolutionizes renewable energy harvesting by capturing the micro-vibrations and kinetic energy generated by human footsteps. Installed in high-footfall environments, WalkWatt seamlessly converts mechanical movement into usable, stored electricity—powering lights, sensors, and micro-grids silently and continuously.

The Wasted Energy Opportunity

Millions of daily footsteps lose power to the ground. We capture it.

High Footfall

Airports, railway stations, malls, and schools see hundreds of thousands of people daily walking over inert flooring.

Unused Potential

Every single step exerts pressure that dissipates into the environment as heat and sound. It is a completely untapped clean energy grid.

Smart Solution

WalkWatt bridges urban foot traffic with micro-power generation, turning everyday movement into off-grid sustainability.
